FAQ

Does the app work offline?

The iOS candidate is designed to bundle the game locally. The web version may need an initial load from GitHub Pages, but gameplay itself does not rely on a backend.

Do I need an account?

No. Pole to Glory has no accounts, login, authentication or online profile system.

Where is progress stored?

Progress such as Build & Race XP, upgrades and Mini Season state is stored locally on your device/browser. It is not uploaded to a server.

How do I reset progress?

Use the in-app reset options for Build & Race or Mini Season where available. You can also clear browser site data or delete the app to remove local progress.

How do Daily Race and Challenge Race work?

They use deterministic seeds so players can manually compare the same fictional race setup. There is no online leaderboard and no score upload.

Is the content based on real racing?

No. Drivers, teams, circuits, racing events and results are fictional. Pole to Glory does not use real-world racing brands, official data or affiliation claims.
