Privacy Policy

Pole to Glory

Effective date: June 14, 2026

Pole to Glory is a fictional racing strategy game designed to be local-first and privacy-conscious.

Back to app

Summary

The current app does not require an account, does not include ads, does not include in-app purchases and does not send gameplay data to a server.

Information We Collect

The current version of Pole to Glory does not collect personal information from users.

Local Game Progress

The app stores game progress locally on your device, such as career XP, upgrades, mini-season progress and saved mode state. This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us.

You can remove local progress by using in-app reset options where available or by deleting the app from your device.

Clipboard

The app can copy result summaries or challenge links to your clipboard when you tap a copy button. The app does not read clipboard contents.

Analytics, Ads and Tracking

The current app does not use analytics, advertising SDKs or tracking technologies.

Accounts and Payments

The current app does not use accounts, subscriptions, in-app purchases or payment processing.

Third-Party Services

The current app does not connect to external gameplay services. If future versions add online features, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

Children’s Privacy

Pole to Glory is not directed specifically to children. The current app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Content and Affiliation

All drivers, teams, circuits and racing situations in Pole to Glory are fictional. The app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any real-world racing championship, team, driver, circuit or governing body.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if the app changes. The effective date will be updated when the policy changes.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the public support page: Pole to Glory Support.
